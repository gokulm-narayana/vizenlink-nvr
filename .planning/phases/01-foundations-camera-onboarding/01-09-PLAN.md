<objective>
Deliver the add slice for a configured camera: the owner adds a camera by IP/host from the CLI, the NVR verifies it, reads its identity and capabilities, selects main and sub streams (with the D-23 fallback), and registers it under its serial in one transaction; list and show display it (CAM-02, CAM-05, CAM-07, CAM-10; D-02, D-03, D-05, D-06, D-11, D-14, D-15, D-16, D-21, D-23).

Purpose: The camera registry keyed by serial and the selected streams are what Phase 2 records from.
Output: migration 0002, registry/streams/onboarding services, add/list/show commands, outcome presentation table, tests. Failure taxonomy, factory flow and atomicity proofs follow in 01-10.

Reversibility flags: D-11 (serial primary key) is one-way and gated by Task 1. D-08 (the NVR uses the camera's `admin` account, no NVR-owned camera account) is costly: switching to a dedicated account later touches every stored credential and needs an owner-visible migration. Keeping `username` as a column of `camera_credential` (not a hard-coded constant at use sites) keeps that migration row-by-row.
</objective>
