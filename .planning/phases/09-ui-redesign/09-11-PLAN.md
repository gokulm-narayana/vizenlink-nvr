Facts verified while planning:
- The legacy page queried the timeline with UTC day bounds (`{date}T00:00:00Z`) while drawing a browser-local day. The new page uses `zonedDayBounds(day, siteZone)` for both (D-15).
- `GET /api/v1/playback/days` computes days as UTC calendar dates (`services/timeline.py`). Footage-day dots therefore follow UTC dates and can be off by one near midnight for non-UTC sites. Keep the endpoint unchanged and record the gap for the owner (a `tz` parameter would be a backend change outside D-14 and D-15).
- Endpoints: `/playback/segment/{id}` (byte-range video), `/playback/timeline`, `/playback/days`, `POST /playback/snapshot {camera_serial, image_base64, title?, captured_at?}`, `POST /playback/export {camera_serial, start_time, end_time, title?}`.
- Only this plan edits `features/playback/` outside `timeline/`.
</context>
